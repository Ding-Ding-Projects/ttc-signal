Rider guide.

Use Signal Toronto to find a stop, track service and understand reported delays. All service times use America/Toronto.

Track a vehicle

Open Live network. The route menu narrows the map to a line. Select a vehicle label to inspect its route and last report. Zoom in to see stops and select one to check departures. Use the location control to center the map on your position after granting browser permission. The network map includes scheduled route geometry, so a drawn line alone does not indicate a vehicle is running.

Read departures and trip times

In Arrivals, enter a stop name or TTC stop number. Open the stop to see surface arrival predictions and the published timetable. Change the date to include the correct weekday service and holiday exceptions. Select a departure to see its stops in order. Times above 24:00 refer to trips after midnight on the selected service day. Accessibility information reflects the published boarding field, which can be unspecified.

Understand delays

Service alerts combines dated GTFS Realtime advisories with surface messages. Check the snapshot time when planning a trip. A missing prediction, position or message can mean the feed has incomplete coverage. Delay history is a separate monthly archive. Choose a mode, month, cause or route and inspect the reported delay minutes and gap. The archive starts in January 2025.

Plan a journey

Search for the exact boarding and destination stops. Select each suggested stop, the service date and the earliest departure time. Results are ordered by scheduled arrival and include direct rides and one same-stop transfer with at least two minutes between trips. Walking links and live delays are outside this calculation.

Inspect real-time records

Open Feed explorer to filter vehicle positions, occupancy reports, trip updates or reported cancellations. Expand a record to see available fields. Check the timestamp on each snapshot before using it for travel decisions.

Personalize your workspace

Drag a dockview tab to move, split or group panels. Reset layout restores the current view. Save a stop or route from its detail screen. Saved items and layouts stay in the current browser. All map stops and routes are also accessible through the searchable directory. Use Tab to move between controls and Escape to close a detail screen.

Data coverage and credits

Signal Toronto is an independent project. Data is supplied by the Toronto Transit Commission and the City of Toronto. Check TTC service advisories for official travel information.

  • TTC routes and schedules supply stops, trips, routes, shapes, stop times, agency details, calendars and date exceptions.
  • TTC GTFS Realtime supplies advisories, trip updates and vehicle observations. The TTC public surface feed supplies browser updates. Live observations depend on agency coverage and network access.
  • Toronto Open Data supplies bus, streetcar and subway delay records and Toronto Centreline geography. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Toronto.
  • Bundled streets, green spaces and waterbodies by the City of Toronto. Optional detailed map tiles by OpenStreetMap contributors. The map also includes bundled street geometry.
