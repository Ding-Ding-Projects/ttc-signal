Station history

  1. January 28, 1978

    The Spadina line opened through the Allen Road median. Rita Letendre’s coloured skylight, Joy, brought daylight into the concrete station.

    transittoronto.ca
  2. 2020

    Rita Letendre reinterpreted Joy for the replacement skylight. Colour sits between layers of glass in the new design, protecting it from the leaks that damaged the original.

    ttc.ca
  3. October 1, 2024

    Elevators entered service at Glencairn, providing step-free subway access.

    ttc.ca
