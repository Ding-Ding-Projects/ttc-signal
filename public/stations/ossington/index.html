Station history

  1. February 26, 1966

    Passenger trains began serving Ossington on the original Bloor–Danforth subway between Keele and Woodbine.

    transittoronto.ca
  2. 2016

    Two elevators entered service in December, making Ossington the TTC’s 35th accessible station.

    ttc.ca
  3. 2016

    Scott Eunson’s Ossington Particles brought clusters of polished Corian shapes to the station walls.

    scotteunson.ca
