McCowan

3 Scarborough RT

Station history

  1. March 22, 1985

    McCowan was the eastern terminus when the Scarborough RT officially opened.

    ttc.ca
  2. July 24, 2023

    The last Line 3 trains ran on this date. A derailment near Ellesmere interrupted service.

    ttc.ca
  3. August 24, 2023

    The TTC confirmed permanent closure of Line 3. McCowan was one of four stations that closed permanently.

    ttc.ca
