Station history

  1. February 26, 1966

    Woodbine opened as the eastern terminus. Riders continued east on a Danforth streetcar shuttle.

    transittoronto.ca
  2. May 11, 1968

    Trains began continuing east to Warden. The temporary streetcar shuttle from Woodbine to Luttrell Loop ended.
