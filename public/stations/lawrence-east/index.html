Lawrence East

3 Scarborough RT

Station history

  1. March 22, 1985

    Lawrence East was one of the six stations on the Scarborough RT at its official opening.

    ttc.ca
  2. July 24, 2023

    The last Line 3 trains ran on this date. A derailment near Ellesmere interrupted service.

    ttc.ca
  3. August 24, 2023

    The TTC confirmed permanent closure of Line 3. Lawrence East was one of four stations that closed permanently.

    ttc.ca
  4. September 30, 2026

    The Scarborough Busway opened along the former rail corridor with a bus stop at Lawrence East.

    ttc.ca
