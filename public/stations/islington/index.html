Station history

  1. May 11, 1968

    Islington became the western terminus when the subway extended beyond Keele.

    transittoronto.ca
  2. November 22, 1980

    Kipling took over as the western terminus. Islington became a through station.

    transittoronto.ca
  3. 1996

    Renovations reorganized the fare barriers and moved the collector booths to simplify transfers to the buses.
