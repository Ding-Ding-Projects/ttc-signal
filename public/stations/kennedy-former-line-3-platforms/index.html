Kennedy (former Line 3 platforms)

3 Scarborough RT

Station history

  1. March 22, 1985

    The Scarborough RT officially opened, giving Kennedy a rail transfer to Scarborough Centre and McCowan.

    ttc.ca
  2. July 24, 2023

    The last Line 3 trains ran on this date. A derailment near Ellesmere interrupted service.

    ttc.ca
  3. August 24, 2023

    The TTC confirmed the end of Line 3 train service. The closure applied to the Line 3 platforms at Kennedy. Subway and bus service continued.

    ttc.ca
  4. February 8, 2026

    Kennedy gained a Line 5 interchange with Line 2 at the operating station.

    ttc.ca
