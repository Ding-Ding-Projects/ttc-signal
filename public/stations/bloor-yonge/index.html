Station history

  1. March 30, 1954

    The original station opened as Bloor. Stairs led from the subway to a sheltered streetcar transfer platform in the middle of Bloor Street.

    transittoronto.caAdditional source
  2. February 26, 1966

    Bloor-Danforth trains replaced the streetcar transfer with a subway interchange at Yonge Street.
