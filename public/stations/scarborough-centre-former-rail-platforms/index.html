Station history

  1. March 22, 1985

    The official opening ceremony for the Scarborough RT took place at Scarborough Centre.

    barp.ca
  2. July 24, 2023

    The last Line 3 trains ran on this date. A derailment near Ellesmere interrupted service.

    ttc.ca
  3. August 24, 2023

    The TTC confirmed permanent closure of the rail line. Scarborough Centre continued serving riders as a bus terminal.
