Station history

  1. January 28, 1978

    Streetcars and buses met the new subway at an underground loop. Gordon Rayner’s enamel mural Tempo was installed on the mezzanine.

    transittoronto.ca
  2. 2016

    Morning rush-hour trains that used to turn back here began running north to Glencairn before reversing direction.

    ttc.ca
  3. 2017

    The TTC completed the station’s accessibility improvements, giving riders a step-free connection to Line 1.
