Station history

  1. 1985

    Construction began in June at the site then known as Park Home. Crews built around the existing tunnel while Yonge trains continued running.

    toronto.ca
  2. June 18, 1987

    The TTC officially opened an infill station between Sheppard and Finch, providing a subway entrance in North York’s civic centre district.
