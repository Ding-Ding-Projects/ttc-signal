Station history

  1. March 30, 1954

    Builders left space under the Yonge platforms for a proposed Queen streetcar subway. Two passenger passages crossed beneath the tracks around that space.

    transittoronto.ca
  2. May 1, 2023

    Traffic moved off Queen Street between Bay and Victoria so crews could build the Ontario Line station that will connect with the existing subway.
