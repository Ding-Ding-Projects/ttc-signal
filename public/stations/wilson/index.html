Station history

  1. January 28, 1978

    Public rides began at the original Spadina line terminus. Its two-level bus terminal gathered suburban routes beside the new Wilson subway yard.

  2. 1996

    The extension to Downsview moved several bus routes farther north and Wilson’s overflow bus terminal closed.
