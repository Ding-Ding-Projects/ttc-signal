Station history

  1. February 26, 1966

    Passenger trains began serving Christie on the original Bloor–Danforth subway between Keele and Woodbine.

    transittoronto.ca
  2. October 15, 1976

    A train fire forced an evacuation and damaged the station. Service between Ossington and St. George was suspended while crews made repairs.
