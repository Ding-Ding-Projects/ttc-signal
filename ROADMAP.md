# Transit coverage

- [x] Agency, service calendars, exceptions, routes, stops, trips, stop times and shapes
- [x] Service alerts, trip updates, reported cancellations and vehicle positions
- [x] Reported occupancy and stop boarding accessibility where supplied
- [x] Scheduled direct and same-stop one-transfer journeys
- [x] Monthly incident reports, route and cause filters, duration and gap metrics
- [x] Persistent dockable workspace and saved routes and stops

## Coverage limits

The current static TTC distribution supplies eight tables. Fare products, pathways, station levels and transfer rules are outside this distribution. Subway trains may have no location observation. The journey planner does not calculate walking transfers, step-free itineraries or paths with multiple transfers. The archive uses the city's current-format datasets starting in January 2025.
